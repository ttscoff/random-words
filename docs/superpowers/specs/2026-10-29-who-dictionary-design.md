# Doctor Who (`who`) Dictionary Design

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Status:** Approved for implementation planning  
**Trigger:** `who` (also `doctorwho`, `tardis`)

## Goal

Add a Doctor Who themed dictionary under `lib/random-words/words/who/`, focused on dialog and characters, discovered like every other source (no generator code changes). Medical `doctor` remains separate and unchanged.

## Decisions

| Topic | Choice |
|-------|--------|
| Era | Mostly New Who (2005–present), light Classic sprinkle |
| Quote density | Medium — solid iconic set; bulk is themed vocabulary |
| Size | Large (~Walken / 2500+ lines total) |
| Character weight | Even spread across NuWho Doctors; Classic icons (Fourth Doctor, Sarah Jane, UNIT, etc.) get more of the sprinkle |
| Approach | Hybrid: names/phrases/clauses carry dialog & characters; nouns/verbs/adjectives supply franchise lexicon bulk |

## Layout

New directory mirroring existing dictionaries:

```
lib/random-words/words/who/
  config.yml
  adjectives.txt
  adverbs.txt
  articles-plural.txt
  articles-singular.txt
  clauses.txt
  conjunctions-coordinating.txt
  conjunctions-subordinate.txt
  names.txt
  nouns-plural.txt
  nouns-singular.txt
  numbers.yml
  phrases.txt
  prepositions.txt
  terminators.txt
  verbs-passive.txt
  verbs-plural.txt
  verbs-singular.txt
```

### config.yml

```yaml
---
name: who
description: Doctor Who (New Who-forward, Classic sprinkle)
triggers: [who, doctorwho, tardis]
```

No changes to `generator.rb` or other Ruby sources; dictionaries are loaded by directory name / triggers.

## Content design

### names.txt (~80–120)

Sentence-friendly character labels:

- NuWho Doctors: Ninth through Fifteenth, The Doctor, War Doctor
- Companions across NuWho (Rose, Martha, Donna, Amy, Rory, Clara, Bill, Yaz, Dan, Ruby, and peers)
- Villains and monsters: Master / Missy, Daleks, Cybermen, Weeping Angels, Silence, and similar
- Classic sprinkle: Fourth Doctor, Sarah Jane, Brigadier, UNIT, K-9

Prefer names that work as subjects (`Rose Tyler`, `The Doctor`, `Donna Noble`) over actor names.

### phrases.txt (~50–70)

Recognizable lines and catchphrases without becoming a quote dump. Mix Doctor catchphrases, companion beats, monster slogans, and a few Classic classics (e.g. jelly babies, Exterminate!).

### clauses.txt (~180–250)

Show-voice fragments used as clause inserts: TARDIS arrivals, regenerations, Dalek/Cyberman threats, timey-wimey explanations, companion loyalty, UNIT briefings. First- and third-person mix (Walken-style), Who-flavored.

### Lexicon bulk (Walken-scale)

| File | Target | Focus |
|------|--------|--------|
| nouns-singular | ~400–500 | TARDIS, sonic screwdriver, regeneration, Gallifrey, Skaro, vortex, paradox, Time Lord, species, tech, places |
| nouns-plural | ~300–400 | Matching plurals |
| verbs-singular / verbs-plural | ~250–300 each | regenerate, dematerialize, exterminate, travel, warn, convert, reverse… |
| verbs-passive | ~200–350 | was regenerated by, was converted by, was chased by… |
| adjectives | ~130–200 | timey-wimey, ancient, doomed, brilliant, impossible… |
| adverbs | ~130–200 | suddenly, brilliantly, bravely, paradoxically… |

Tone: adventurous, ominous, witty. Not medical vocabulary.

### Structural files

Articles, coordinating/subordinate conjunctions, prepositions, terminators, and `numbers.yml` copied from `english` or `walken` with little or no Who flavor (same pattern as other themed dictionaries).

### Size target

Roughly **2500+ lines** across all word files combined.

## Out of scope

- Changes to sentence templates or generator logic
- Renaming or altering the medical `doctor` dictionary
- Torchwood / Sarah Jane Adventures as full separate dictionaries (individual characters may appear in `names` where familiar)
- Exhaustive quote catalogs or copyrighted script dumps beyond short iconic phrases

## Verification

1. `RandomWords::Generator.new(:who)` initializes without error
2. Trigger aliases (`who`, `doctorwho`, `tardis`) resolve to this source
3. Sample sentences/paragraphs feel Who-themed (characters, tech, dialog fragments appear)
4. Line counts land near the targets above (especially names/phrases/clauses and noun/verb bulk)

## Implementation notes

- Reuse structural lists from `english`/`walken` to avoid reinventing grammar glue
- Prefer franchise-common terms over obscure one-off episode props unless they are culturally iconic
- Keep singular/plural and singular/plural verb pairs consistent where both files exist
